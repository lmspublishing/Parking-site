Free and cheap parking around Bondi

Bondi parking can vary sharply by street, time and stay length. The finder combines mapped parking with integrated Waverley parking data and selected current off-street tariffs.

Evidence before a “free” label

Older public street-bay data can be useful for finding candidates, but it is not treated as proof that a space is currently free. Those results stay low-confidence and direct you to check the physical sign.

Use it to compare

  • walking distance to the beach, shops or your destination;
  • current integrated car-park tariffs; and
  • mapped sign context where available.
