Why use it?

Budget first

Known $0 periods and lower-cost public parking are easy to compare against walking distance instead of being buried behind booking inventory.

Evidence first

Uncertain does not mean free

When the available data cannot support a price or rule for your selected stay, the result is kept as “check sign” rather than guessed.

Sydney focused

Current council rules can matter more than generic map data

The app increasingly uses authoritative council tariffs, time limits and facility rules to correct or supplement generic mapped parking.

Coverage is useful, but not universal.

Public-data coverage is strongest in areas where current council or Transport sources have been integrated, including City of Sydney, Waverley/Bondi, Willoughby/Chatswood, North Sydney/Crows Nest, Inner West, Lane Cove, Woollahra, Ku-ring-gai, Ryde, Burwood, Parramatta, Strathfield, Blacktown, Northern Beaches, Bayside, Randwick and Georges River, plus supported Transport Park&Ride and mapped OpenStreetMap parking. An absent result does not prove there is no parking nearby. Street signs and temporary restrictions always override the app.

Questions

Does the app guarantee a legal parking space?

No. It helps compare available public data and mapped parking. Drivers must always check the physical sign and any temporary restriction before parking.

Is “free parking” always free?

No. Results are only labelled as confirmed/free where the available rule data supports that for the selected stay. Other mapped parking is shown as “check” rather than guessed.

Is Park&Ride free?

Transport Park&Ride eligibility is conditional. The app does not treat it as unconditional $0 parking, and live space counts are estimates rather than reservations.

Is Sydney Parking Finder free?

Yes. The public beta is free to use and does not require a booking or payment to compare parking options.

Does the site track where I search?

The beta’s operational event logging is designed not to record typed addresses, destination coordinates, Maps URLs, IP addresses, user IDs or session IDs.
